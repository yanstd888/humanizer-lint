    How it works
    A closed loop for prose quality
    Measure the completed draft before publishing it.
    1. Lexical and copywriting rules
    Checks eight lexical packs and selected copy or CTA phrases.
    2. Sentence and paragraph rhythm
    Measures CV and adjacent delta; rhythm gates require eight sentences.
    3. Vocabulary and n-grams
    Uses adaptive MATTR, repeated n-grams, and Indonesian morphology.
    4. Domain hygiene
    File-aware CLI checks include UI accessibility, fixed widths, and code comments; the playground accepts plain text.
    5. Delivery Gate
    Review four diagnostic blocks and preserve meaning over scores.
    

    Multilingual coverage
    Eight anti-cliché packs, plus universal rhythm diagnostics
    A local AI humanizer and prose linter for revising robotic writing, repeated stock phrases, flat sentence rhythm, Markdown, copywriting and calls to action. It checks style, not authorship.
    A local, zero-dependency prose linter that flags stiff, AI-sounding writing (stock phrases, flat sentence rhythm, repeated wording) and tells you what to revise. A style checker, not an AI detector. Experimental rhythm diagnostics (uncalibrated thresholds). Humanizer-Lint is a prose linter, writing-style editor, stylometric analyzer, and deterministic local CLI validator. It reports sentence rhythm, paragraph rhythm, adjacent sentence delta, moving-average type-token ratio (MATTR), repeated n-grams, and compression ratio as diagnostics while retaining private local analysis.

    FAQ
    Can this tool prove human authorship?
    No. It checks configured writing-style signals, not who wrote the text.
    Does the playground send text to a server?
    No. Analysis is client-side and the CLI has no runtime network dependency.
    Which languages are supported?
    Eight lexical packs cover EN, ID, ES, MS, FR, DE, PT, and ZH. Rhythm diagnostics for other languages are experimental and uncalibrated.
