Subject: Could we try a shared agenda?

Hi everyone,

I have a small idea for our weekly meeting. What if we kept the agenda in one shared document for a month? I am not assuming the current meetings are broken; I just think a short trial would tell us whether a little preparation makes them easier to use.

The person hosting could add each topic before the call, along with the question we need to answer. Anyone else could add a note or flag something we have missed. During the meeting, we could write down decisions and follow-up owners in the same place, so nobody has to piece them together from chat afterward. If a topic can be settled in writing, we can leave it there and give the meeting time back.

After four meetings, let's ask the team what worked and what felt like extra admin. Did people have enough context? Could they find the decisions afterward? Did writing the agenda take longer than it saved? We can make a call from those answers rather than committing to another permanent process now.

I can set up the first document. Please reply if you are happy to try it or if there is a better way to run the experiment.

Thanks.
