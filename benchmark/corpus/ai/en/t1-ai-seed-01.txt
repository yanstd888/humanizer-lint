Subject: A four-week trial for meeting agendas

Hi team,

I would like to try a shared agenda for our weekly meetings for four weeks. The aim is simple: make it easier to see what needs a decision and give everyone a chance to prepare. This is a proposal, not a response to any measured problem.

Before each meeting, the host could post a short document with the topics, the person raising each topic, and the decision or discussion needed. Team members could add context or questions in advance. During the meeting, the host would keep notes next to each item and record who volunteered for any follow-up. Items that do not need live discussion can stay in the document instead.

At the end of the trial, we could ask whether the format helped people prepare, whether any decisions were easier to track, and whether maintaining the document took too much time. We should also check if anyone found it harder to participate. Based on that feedback, we can keep the practice, change it, or stop.

Would you be willing to try this at our next meeting? I can create the first agenda and share it beforehand.

Thanks.
