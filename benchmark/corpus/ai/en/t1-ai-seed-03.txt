Subject: Agenda experiment for the next month

Hello team,

Could we test one change to our weekly call? For the next four meetings, I suggest putting discussion topics in a shared page before we join. The goal is to make the purpose of each conversation visible and leave a record of what we decide. We do not yet know whether the change will help, so I would like to treat it as an experiment.

Each person could add a topic and say whether they need a decision, a quick update, or help from someone else. The host could order the items and leave space for notes. During the call, we would write the decision and any agreed next step beside the relevant topic. A question that can be resolved in the document would not need to take time on the call.

After the fourth meeting, we can collect brief feedback. I suggest asking whether people felt prepared, whether notes were useful later, and whether maintaining the page was worth the effort. We should also invite suggestions from anyone who found the format awkward or less accessible.

If there is interest, I will make a first draft for the next call. If not, we can find another approach together.
